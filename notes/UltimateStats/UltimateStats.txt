FORMULAS
- Duration:      20 + 1.5 * (level - 1) seconds
- Air Strike:    4 + (level - 1) strikes
- Leech:         3 + (level - 1) health per strike
- Time Slow:     enemy speed = max(10%, 60% - 4.5% * (level - 1)). Hits the 10% floor at level 13
- Aim Assist:    math range = [ceil(level / 2) below, floor(level / 2) above], so it covers `level` extra answers
                 typing max score = level (see word_mistake_score)
- Collateral:    1.5 ring widths (~51.5px) + 24px * (level - 1)
- Turret:        2 * level turrets, max 7 per orbital
- Rings:         same as the Aim Assist math range, but in rings

TYPING SCORE CHEAT SHEET (Aim Assist)
Mistake kinds: extra/missing letter, swapped neighbor letters, wrong letter.
Each kind with n mistakes is worth 3^(n-1), and the score is the sum of the kinds.
- 1 mistake                             = 1   (home -> homes, hoem, hime)
- 2 mistakes, different kinds           = 2   (home -> hoems)
- 2 mistakes, same kind                 = 3   (home -> came, ohem)
- 3 mistakes, all different kinds       = 3
- 2 of one kind + 1 of another          = 4
- 3 of the same kind                    = 9
Scores of 10 or 20 at high levels match almost any two short words.
