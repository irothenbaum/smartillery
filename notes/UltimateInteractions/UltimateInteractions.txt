WHAT EACH DAMAGE SOURCE GIVES YOU
Source of truth: handle_enemy_hit (scripts/scr_events) and its callers. Update this note when those change.

                                  Streak   Combo   Points    Ult charge   Triggers   Triggers
                                                   on kill   on kill      Leech      Collateral + Rings
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your typed answer                 +1       yes     yes       yes          yes        yes
Aim Assist extra targets          no       yes     yes       yes          yes        yes
Air Strike bombs                  no       yes     yes       yes          yes        yes
Turret ult beams                  no       yes     yes       yes          yes        no
Collateral chain                  no       yes     yes       yes          yes        no
Rings                             no       yes     yes       yes          yes        no
Stationary turret (power item)    no       no      yes       no           no         no
Ship explosion (taking damage)    no       no      no        no           no         no
Shooting your own item (0XX)      +1       yes     n/a       n/a          no         no   (no turret shots or Aim Assist either)
Leech, Time Slow                  (don't damage enemies)


RULES
- Streak only goes up when your typed answer hits its target directly (handle_submit_code -> increase_streak).
  A wrong answer resets it, unless Aim Assist finds a near-match (then the streak is left unchanged).
- Combo only counts while you're on streak. It counts HITS, not kills (a Ram is 3 hits), and every hit restarts the
  combo timer. Maxing out the combo fully charges your ult (not while ulting) and always drops a power item.
- Points go to whoever hit the enemy last (last_hit_by_player_id). Ult charge too, only while on streak, and only if
  the killing blow's source grants it (last_hit_grants_ult_charge).
- Air Strike counts as a player shot on purpose, so its bombs set off Collateral and Rings.
- Collateral and Rings hits aren't "player shots", so they can't chain into more Collateral or Rings.
- The ship explosion hits each enemy once for 1 damage (knockback/stun as normal). A kill from it rewards no one,
  and an enemy it doesn't kill keeps its last hitter (enemy_hit_without_reward).


HANDLE_ENEMY_HIT OPTIONS (4th argument, a struct)
  from_player_shot      marks Aim Assist targets + triggers Collateral and Rings   (default false)
  counts_toward_combo   adds to combo while on streak                             (default true)
  triggers_leech        drops a Leech health orb                                  (default: from_player_shot)
  grants_ult_charge     a kill gives ult charge while on streak                   (default true)

Callers:
  obj_player fire_at_instance (typed answers + Aim Assist)   {from_player_shot: true}
  obj_ultimate_strike                                         {from_player_shot: true}
  obj_ult_turret_turret                                       {triggers_leech: true}
  obj_ultimate_collateral                                     {triggers_leech: true}
  obj_ultimate_rings                                          {triggers_leech: true}
  obj_stationary_turret                                       {counts_toward_combo: false, triggers_leech: false, grants_ult_charge: false}
